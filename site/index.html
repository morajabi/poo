On a developer Mac the space doesn't go to photos. It goes to DerivedData, Rust target folders, node_modules, simulator runtimes and the worktrees your coding agents leave behind, each with its own copy of everything.

Poo knows about 650 of these places. It checks what's running, what you touched recently and what has uncommitted work, then cleans only what nothing is using. You review every clean, and worktrees are backed up first.

It lives in your menu bar, runs in your terminal, and gives your agent an MCP server, so Claude Code or Codex can check your disk and clean with you.
